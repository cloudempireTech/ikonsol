About Ikonsol Edutech Pvt. Limited

Learning with a wider purpose.

Ikonsol Edutech Pvt. Limited aspires to be an internationally recognized educational institute that supports inclusive, equitable quality education and lifelong learning.

The institute describes a focus on teacher training, developing capable educators, and building a centre of excellence for higher learning and research. Its stated aim is to share knowledge that helps shape a knowledge society.
